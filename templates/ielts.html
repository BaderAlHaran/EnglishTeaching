Why there is no band score

Examiners mark IELTS Writing on four criteria, each worth a quarter of your band. This checker measures the parts that can be measured the same way every time. The rest needs a person who reads your answer.

Examiners mark What this checker measures What needs a person
Task Response Word count against the minimum, wording copied from the question, an overview and opinions in Task 1 Whether you answered the question and developed your ideas
Coherence and Cohesion Paragraph count, a concluding paragraph, linking words at the start of paragraphs Whether your argument flows and your ideas connect
Lexical Resource Contractions, informal words, overused phrases, repeated words, spelling The range and precision of your vocabulary
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Grammar and punctuation mistakes The variety of your sentence structures

A band score needs a trained examiner, or software tested against thousands of examiner-marked scripts. Students often get very different numbers from different tools for the same essay, so we would rather show you what is certain and leave the band to the examiner. If you want a judgement on the parts a computer cannot measure, a real person can read your answer for a flat $25.

Frequently asked questions

Does the IELTS writing checker give a band score?

No. A reliable band score needs a trained examiner, or software tested against a large set of examiner-marked scripts. This checker sticks to what can be measured dependably: length, paragraphing, formal language, grammar and spelling. If you want a person to read your answer, you can request a human review for a flat $25.

Will my answer be published?

No. Nothing you paste is published on this site, and there is no public library of essays. Your answer is used only to produce your feedback, and checks are deleted automatically after 30 days. Grammar checking uses the LanguageTool service, so the text is sent there for analysis, as explained in our privacy policy.

Do words copied from the question count towards the word count?

No. Examiners do not count wording copied directly from the question, so an answer that looks long enough can still fall short. Paste the question into the question box and the checker shows how many of your words are copied and how many count.

Is there a maximum word count in IELTS Writing?

No. Writing far more than the minimum does not earn extra marks and leaves less time to check your work. Task 2 counts for twice as much as Task 1, so do not let Task 1 eat into your 40 minutes for Task 2.

Can I use contractions in IELTS Writing?

Avoid them in Task 2 and in Academic Task 1, where examiners expect a formal style: write do not instead of don't, and it is instead of it's. General Training Task 1 letters are different, because an informal letter to a friend can use contractions. That is why this checker does not cover letters yet.

How accurate is the word count?

It counts words and numbers the way most word counters do, so 45% and 2010 each count as one word. Examiners may treat hyphenated words and symbols slightly differently, so aim comfortably above the minimum rather than exactly on it.

Is the IELTS writing checker free?

Yes, with no sign-up. Your text is used only to generate feedback and is not published. Grammar checking uses the LanguageTool service, as explained in our privacy policy.
